# Crapculator support

Crapculator is a calculator with opinions. It runs on iPhone and iPad.

## The calculator refuses to do what I ask

Working as intended. New policies announce themselves as you trip over them, and the Grievances drawer keeps a list of everything you have discovered so far. Every round can be solved. The audit department has checked.

## Something is actually broken

If the app crashes, or a round genuinely cannot be solved, [open an issue](https://github.com/jdlarssen/crapculator-support/issues). Say which device, iOS version and app version/build you have, and what happened. Issues are public; do not post passwords or payment details. Bug reports are processed in the order management deems appropriate.

## Purchases and progress

The first 10 rounds are free. The optional one-time purchase, The Raise, unlocks the remaining campaign and future chapters. Open Grievances → THE RAISE to see the offer or use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to buy it. Purchase access and saved progress are separate: restoring a purchase does not bring back your round progress.

To try a new version, update without deleting the app. Deleting it removes its local save.

## Privacy

Crapculator has no ads or in-app tracking. Progress is stored locally; optional purchases use Apple's services. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
